Mapping & Content

One physical room, many supplier names

Every supplier rate comes back with its own room name. With roomMapping: true, LiteAPI adds a mappedRoomId that points to one master room in the hotel's content. This page shows that resolution live: which supplier names were mapped to which master room, what stayed unmapped, and where the mapping might be wrong.

How to read it

Rate coverage: share of supplier rates carrying a mappedRoomId.
Name variants: distinct supplier names resolved into the same master room.
Check flags: a simple keyword heuristic of mine (bed type, room class, view), not Nuitee's logic. A flag means "worth a human look", not "wrong".
